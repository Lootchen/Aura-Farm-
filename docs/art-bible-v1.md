# Aura Farm — Art Bible v1

## Core tone

**Functional minimalism + bio-industrial music machine.**

Gameplay elements stay simple and high-contrast. Environmental identity lives behind and around them.

Primary rule:

> If the player must read it quickly, simplify it. If it belongs to the world, layer it behind gameplay.

## Detail hierarchy

1. **Primary gameplay** — Tap notes, projectiles, claws, Slide: very low detail.
2. **Secondary gameplay** — CHAIN, Power, bumpers, boss: low/medium detail.
3. **Machine** — chassis, reactor, mechanisms: medium detail.
4. **Environment** — glasshouse, growth bays, conduits: richer but low contrast.
5. **Meta/UI** — AURI, modules, summary: controlled medium detail.

## Palette

Base screen: dark blue-black neutrals.

- Left / technical energy: cyan.
- Right / Aura energy: violet.
- Reward / Power / synergy: gold.
- Ricochet / kinetic: turquoise.
- Pierce / rupture: pink.
- Maximum clarity: white.

No more than 2–3 saturated accents should compete in ordinary gameplay.

## Glow

Glow is feedback, not the default art style.

Use glow for:
- active interactions;
- PERFECT;
- CHAIN;
- Power;
- boss phase changes;
- synergy completion.

Objects must remain readable without glow.

## Tap notes

Incoming Tap notes use only:

**outer ring → dark body → bright core.**

No mechanical casing, internal ornament or highlight stack.

After contact the note becomes a filled projectile, making the transformation visually obvious.

## Projectiles

Projectiles are simple bright balls with a short trail.

Only one extra visual mark per special rule:

- ricochet: turquoise arc;
- pierce: pink axial line;
- Power: gold/white halo.

## Slide

Slide is a continuous musical object:

- clean dark rail;
- one colored edge;
- thin center line;
- full-beat frets;
- tiny half-beat dots;
- one catcher;
- visible judgement corridor.

Do not stack anchor circles, direction arrows and decorative nodes on top of frets.

## Claws / controls

The claws and joysticks should read by silhouette first.

- dark mechanical body;
- one functional color accent;
- minimal material highlight;
- stronger glow only while active.

## Environment

Identity should be added with background layers:

- distant glasshouse frame;
- growth bays;
- machinery silhouettes;
- glass seams;
- low-contrast conduits;
- subtle botanical geometry.

These layers must stay below gameplay contrast.

## “Farm”

Do not interpret Farm as literal agriculture.

Farm means:

- cultivating energy;
- growing builds;
- growing music;
- waking a living machine.

Use sprouts, leaves, roots/circuits, cells and glasshouse architecture sparingly.

## AURI

AURI belongs in the lower central cockpit between both thumbs.

She should not occupy the primary playfield.

Recognisable silhouette:
- hood/helmet;
- visor;
- side pods;
- sprout antenna.

Her reactions communicate gameplay state and left/right physical input.

## HUD

Priority:

1. combo / FLOW;
2. musical section;
3. track progress;
4. act / judgement;
5. score.

The HUD should disappear from attention until needed.

## Aura Modules

Modules are dark hardware objects with one family accent.

Hierarchy:

1. family / route state, very small;
2. large animated behavior preview;
3. module name;
4. short effect;
5. one-sentence explanation.

Do not expose serial numbers or decorative pseudo-technical text as primary information.

The preview should teach the mechanic visually.

## Typography

Use technical display type only for short labels and values.

Use a clean sans for explanatory text.

If text is too small to matter, remove it.

## Feedback hierarchy

PERFECT < CHAIN < Slide PERFECT / Power Return < Synergy < Armor Break < CORE BREAK.

Not every action gets maximum shake/glow.

## Music / world response

Music should animate the environment, not distort note readability.

- kick → chassis pulse;
- harmony → ambience;
- lead → conduits;
- Aura → growth/particles;
- boss → reactor.

## Acceptance tests

### Blur test
Gameplay, HUD and background must still separate when blurred.

### Silhouette test
Notes, claws, AURI and boss must remain recognisable without color.

### Greyscale test
Core gameplay cannot depend only on hue.

### Two-second test
A new viewer should identify what must be hit/followed within two seconds.

## Identity sentence

> An elegant bio-industrial music machine where a small operator cultivates energy, music and builds through two physical claws.

Keywords:

**clean · tactile · musical · kinetic · cultivated · strange · elegant**

Avoid:

**busy · noisy · generic sci-fi · HUD-heavy · rainbow neon**
